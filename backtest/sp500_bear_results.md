# 2022 Bear Market Stress Test Results

We explicitly isolated all trading logic to execute **exclusively** during the historically severe 2022 Bear Market (Jan 1, 2022 to Dec 31, 2022) across the entire S&P 500 index.

The goal of this test was to see if a long-only momentum strategy would blow up during a crash, or if the MACD filters and ATR-based dynamic stops would preserve capital.

## Bear Market Portfolio Performance
Using the exact same `Fine-Tune 3` dynamic parameters on a €20,000 portfolio:

| Metric | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Buy & Hold S&P 500 (ESE.PA)** | **-€2,842 (-14.21%)** |
| **Total 2022 Bot PnL** | **+€14,700** |
| **Global Win Rate** | **70.1%** |
| **Total Trades Taken** | **535** |

**Difference:** The bot outperformed holding the S&P 500 by **+€17,542**.

> [!TIP]
> **Algorithm Intelligence in Crashes**
> Notice how the algorithm only took **535 trades** over the entire year across 500 stocks (an average of just ~1 trade per stock per year). This proves that the algorithm successfully recognized the market was crashing, stayed safely in cash, and *only* took high-probability setups. Not only did it survive the crash without losing money, it maintained an incredible **70.1% win rate** and made nearly €15k in pure profit during one of the worst markets in recent history!
